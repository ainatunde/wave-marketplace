# Nigerian Setup Guide

This document provides comprehensive guides for integrating various services in the Nigerian context for the Wave Marketplace.

## Paystack Integration
- **Step 1:** Sign up at [Paystack](https://paystack.com) and create a new project.
- **Step 2:** Obtain your API keys from the dashboard.
- **Step 3:** Integrate the Paystack SDK into your application, following the [official documentation](https://paystack.com/docs/).

## Flutterwave Integration
- **Step 1:** Register on [Flutterwave](https://www.flutterwave.com) and create an account.
- **Step 2:** Set up your business profile and obtain your API keys.
- **Step 3:** Follow the integration guide at [Flutterwave Docs](https://developer.flutterwave.com/docs).

## Escrow System Setup
- An escrow system holds funds from buyers until they confirm satisfaction with a transaction. 
- **Step 1:** Choose an escrow service provider.
- **Step 2:** Ensure the service supports integration with your platform.
- **Step 3:** Develop the backend logic to handle escrow transactions securely.

## KYC Verification
- Know Your Customer (KYC) requires obtaining and verifying the identity of your users. 
- **Step 1:** Decide on the KYC process (document submission, video verification, etc.).
- **Step 2:** Integrate with KYC service providers.
- **Step 3:** Ensure compliance with legal standards for customer identity verification.

## Multi-Currency Support
- **Step 1:** Assess currency requirements and select a service that supports multiple currencies.
- **Step 2:** Implement currency conversion features within your application.

## Logistics Integration
- Integrate logistics solutions to manage product deliveries efficiently.
- **Step 1:** Choose a logistics partner that operates in Nigeria.
- **Step 2:** Integrate their API for shipment tracking and management.
- **Step 3:** Implement features for users to choose delivery options during checkout.

## Conclusion
This comprehensive setup guide will help you successfully integrate various services required for a robust and efficient marketplace in Nigeria.
